Privacy policy

Castells en vena · Last updated: 22 July 2026

This policy explains how the current version of Castells en vena, a free app with no user accounts, processes personal data.

1. Controller

Controller: Andreu Huguet, established in Catalonia, Spain.

Privacy contact: tenimaletaapp@gmail.com.

No data protection officer has been appointed because this is not mandatory given the current nature and scale of the processing.

2. Data processed

Data stored only on the device

  • Calculator conversations, including their titles and messages.
  • Local copies of the agenda and Hour by Hour content to improve availability and offline reading.
  • App preferences and a random installation identifier. It is not an advertising identifier and is not used to track you across apps or websites.

Conversations can be deleted individually. Other local data is removed when the app is uninstalled or its data is deleted through the operating system.

Calculator queries

When you submit a query, the app sends the backend no more than the 12 most recent messages needed to understand the context, together with the random installation identifier. The backend processes the query and sends it to the configured artificial intelligence provider. Castells en vena does not store these conversations on its backend.

Do not enter personal data. The calculator only needs information about castells to answer.

Technical data

When you connect to the service, the IP address, date and time, requested path, response status and technical information strictly required to provide the service, limit abuse and diagnose incidents may be processed.

Notifications

If you voluntarily enable notifications, iOS manages the permission and Apple may assign an Apple Push Notification service (APNs) token to the device. In the current version, the Castells en vena backend does not receive or retain this token. You can disable notifications in the app or in iOS Settings.

Support communications

If you voluntarily email the contact address, the email address, message contents and any files you choose to attach will be processed. The current app does not automatically export or send conversations to support.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the requested service: displaying content and answering calculator queries. The legal basis is performance of the service requested by the user.
  • Security and stability: limiting abusive requests, preventing fraud and diagnosing errors. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in protecting and maintaining the service, balanced against users' rights.
  • Optional features and support: managing notifications you enable and information you choose to send to support. The legal basis is your consent or voluntary action, which you may withdraw at any time.

Data needed to answer a query and protect the service is required to provide those features. Notifications and contacting support are optional.

4. Providers and recipients

  • Vercel: backend hosting and execution. The primary function is configured in Paris (cdg1), although Vercel and its subprocessors may process data in other countries.
  • OpenAI: linguistic interpretation of calculator queries through the API. Requests are sent with response storage disabled (store: false).
  • Apple: app distribution, system permissions and APNs when you enable notifications.
  • Google/Gmail: receiving and managing emails you voluntarily send to the support or privacy contact.

Revista Castells and the Coordinadora de Colles Castelleres de Catalunya (CCCC) do not receive queries, identifiers or user profiles. The app only displays attributed content and links to their official sources.

5. International transfers

Some providers or subprocessors may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable, these transfers rely on adequacy decisions, European Commission standard contractual clauses or other safeguards recognised by the GDPR. You may request further information through the privacy contact.

6. Retention

  • Local data: until you delete each conversation or uninstall the app.
  • Rate limiter: technical keys are kept for a 10-minute window.
  • Vercel logs: approximately 1 day under the current plan.
  • OpenAI: API data is not used to train models by default; OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days unless a legal obligation requires a different period.
  • Support or privacy emails: for up to 12 months after the query is resolved, unless longer retention is needed to meet a legal obligation or defend legal claims.

7. Advertising, analytics and automated decisions

There is no advertising, cross-app or cross-site tracking, commercial profiling, or additional analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The calculator automates interpretation and scoring, but it does not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on people.

8. External links

When you open a link to Revista Castells, CCCC, Google Maps or another external service, the destination service processes the connection under its own privacy policy. Castells en vena does not control that processing.

9. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability of your data, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing, by emailing tenimaletaapp@gmail.com. Requests will generally be answered within one month.

Because there are no accounts, much of the information exists only on your device and cannot be accessed by the controller. Additional technical information may be required to locate a request without mistakenly identifying another person.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

10. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 14. If a person under that age provides personal data where consent is the applicable legal basis, authorisation from their legal representative is required.

11. Security and changes

Proportionate measures are applied, including HTTPS communications, restricted infrastructure access and no first-party chat persistence on the backend. No system is completely infallible.

Material changes will be published at these same URLs and the date will be updated. If a change requires consent, it will be requested before the change applies.
